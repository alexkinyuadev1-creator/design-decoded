Project Overview

About This Project

This project focused on designing a business card for Simfix Phone Repair that could effectively introduce the business while communicating its core services and contact information. The design uses a dark technology-inspired visual direction with contrasting blue and yellow accents, creating a professional look that reflects the technical nature of the business.

Creating More Than Just A Business card

The goal was to create more than a card containing contact details. The design needed to serve as a compact representation of the Simfix Phone Repair brand while making its services, location, and contact information easy to discover. The combination of the repair-focused logo, technical imagery, strong contrast, and organized layout creates a business card that is both functional and visually distinctive.

The Challenge

The Problem To Solve

Simfix Phone Repair needed a business card that could present the business professionally while communicating its range of repair and electronics services. The challenge was to fit essential information—including services, phone number, contact name, and location—into a compact format without sacrificing readability or visual appeal.

Problem

Present multiple services and essential business information within a compact, professional business card layout.

Goal

Create a memorable business card that communicates Simfix Phone Repair’s services and provides customers with clear contact and location information.

Audience

Customers looking for phone repairs, phone accessories, and home electronics repair services, particularly individuals and local customers within the local area.

Outcome

A professional and informative business card that gives Simfix Phone Repair a distinctive visual presence while making essential customer information easy to access.

Project Goals

Objectives That Guided The Design

Every design decision was guided by the need to balance professional presentation with practical communication. The business card needed to make the Simfix Phone Repair identity recognizable, communicate its services clearly, and ensure that customers could quickly find the information needed to get in touch with the business.

Srengthen Brand Identity

Create a distinctive visual identity for the business card using the Simfix Phone Repair logo, technical imagery, and a consistent blue, yellow, and dark colour direction.

Communicate Services Clearly

Present the phone repair, accessories, and home electronics services in a structured format that customers can understand at a glance.

Prioritize Essential Information

Make the phone number, contact name, location, and other key business details easy to locate and read.

Create a Professional Impression

Develop a polished business card that communicates reliability and professionalism while giving customers something visually memorable to associate with the business.
