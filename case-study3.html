Project Overview

About This Project

This project focused on creating a sophisticated birthday social media design for Emilia Muthoni’s 33rd birthday celebration. The visual direction was built around elegance, warmth, and personal expression, combining a rich burgundy-red background with gold typography, a carefully positioned portrait, and refined decorative details to create a design that feels both celebratory and premium.

Creating More Than Just A Birthday Post

The goal was to create more than a simple birthday greeting. The design needed to make the celebration feel personal, elegant, and memorable while keeping the birthday message and age immediately visible. The combination of rich red tones, gold accents, expressive typography, portrait photography, and flowing decorative lines creates a visual experience that celebrates both the occasion and the individual.

The Challenge

The Problem To Solve

The challenge was to transform a simple birthday message into a visually distinctive social media design that feels worthy of a milestone celebration. The design needed to balance personal photography with elegant typography and decorative elements while ensuring that the birthday message, name, and age remained clear and visually dominant.

Project Goals

Objectives That Guided The Design

Every design decision was guided by the need to make the birthday celebration feel personal and visually memorable. The composition was carefully structured to establish a clear hierarchy between the birthday message, the celebrant’s name, portrait, age, and supporting details while using colour and decorative elements to create an elegant celebratory atmosphere.

Create a First Strong Impression

Use bold yet elegant typography and a rich visual composition to immediately communicate the importance of the birthday celebration.

Celebrate the Individual

Make the celebrant the visual focus by integrating the portrait naturally into the composition while allowing the surrounding elements to support rather than compete with the image.

Establish an Elegant Aesthetic

Combine deep red tones, warm gold accents, refined typography, and subtle decorative details to create a sophisticated and premium birthday atmosphere.

Create a Memorable Social Graphic

Develop a visually distinctive design that feels personal and shareable while remaining clear and effective across social media platforms.
