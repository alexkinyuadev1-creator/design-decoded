Project Overview

About This Project

This project focused on designing a compelling event flyer for King’s Tabernacle International’s Thanksgiving celebration. The goal was to combine a strong church-focused visual direction with clear event information, creating a design that feels celebratory, welcoming, and spiritually meaningful across both digital and print platforms.

Creating More Than Just An Event Flyer

The goal was to create more than a visually attractive church flyer. The design needed to capture the spirit of thanksgiving, communicate the significance of the celebration, and encourage people to attend. A warm orange palette, strong typography, expressive imagery, and carefully structured event details work together to create a flyer that feels energetic, welcoming, and appropriate for a major church gathering.

The Challenge

The Problem To Solve

The church needed an event flyer that could communicate the Thanksgiving celebration clearly while capturing the energy and spiritual atmosphere of the gathering. The design had to make important information easy to find, create an immediate sense of occasion, and encourage members and visitors to participate

Problem

The event needed a visually compelling promotional flyer that could attract attention and communicate the celebration effectively.

Goal

Create a bold and welcoming event flyer that clearly presents the Thanksgiving celebration, theme, date, time, venue, and other essential information.

Audience

Church members
Families
Visitors
Young adults
Members of the wider community who may be interested in attending the celebration.

Outcome

A professional and engaging church event flyer that communicates the event clearly while creating a strong visual invitation to attend.

Project Goals

Objectives That Guided The Design

Every design decision was guided by the purpose of the church event. Rather than focusing on decoration alone, the flyer was designed to attract attention, communicate the event details quickly, reflect the celebratory nature of Thanksgiving, and create a clear invitation for people to attend.

Capture Attention

Create a bold visual composition that immediately draws attention and makes the Thanksgiving celebration stand out across digital and printed formats.

Communicate Clearly

Organize the date, time, venue, theme, and other event information so viewers can understand the details quickly and effortlessly.

Reflect the Event

Use imagery, colour, typography, and composition to communicate the warmth, joy, gratitude, and celebratory atmosphere of the church gathering.

Encourage Attendance

Create an inviting visual experience that generates interest in the event and encourages church members, families, and visitors to participate.
